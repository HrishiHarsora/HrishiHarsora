I am a senior undergraduate studying Materials Science at IIT Gandhinagar.  

My interests lie in modelling deposition processes used to fabricate logic and memory. 

Currently, I am a **visiting research fellow at Caltech** working on probabilistic modelling of ALD at experimentally relevant length and time scales.
